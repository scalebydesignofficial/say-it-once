# Talking points

A cheat sheet the person can pull from for anything else: a Live, Stories, a podcast guest spot, a workshop, a DM reply, a comment, a video they decide to record. One file per source.

## Where it goes
`talking-points.md` in the source folder.

## Shape
```
# Talking points: working title of the source

## The big idea
One sentence. Then two or three sentences on why it matters to this audience.

## Teaching points
1. Point in plain words.
   - Say it like this: one or two sentences in their voice.
   - From the source: a short real line (exact, 20 words or fewer) if there is one.
   - Good for: Live / Story / carousel / email (pick the best fit)
2. ...

## Stories and examples
Only what is really in the source, each in two or three lines. If there are none, write "None in this source."

## Beliefs this challenges
- The belief people hold → what the teaching says instead

## Questions to ask your audience
5 to 8 specific questions that start a real conversation. Not "What do you think?"

## Likely objections, and how to answer
- "Objection in their words." → short answer in the person's voice

## Hooks you can open with
8 to 10 first lines, each a different style (challenge, confession, question, plain truth, permission).

## If you only have 60 seconds
A 100 to 130 word version they could say out loud right now.

## Prompts for going live
3 quick prompts to riff on if they open Instagram Live or a Facebook Live with no script.

## Your next step for them
The one invitation, exactly as in brand/offer.md.
```

## Rules
- Everything comes from the source. No invented stories, numbers, or quotes. Anything that needs the person's own experience goes in as a question to them: "Do you have a story about this? Put it here."
- Short lines they can glance at mid-recording.
- Their voice, from `brand/voice.md`.
