# Talking reel scripts

Scripts the person records on camera, or pastes into an AI avatar tool such as HeyGen, or reads from a teleprompter.

## Where it goes
`reels/reel-NN.md` and `reels/reel-NN.spoken.txt`

## `reel-NN.md`
```
# Reel NN: working title
Length: about 30 seconds (80 to 90 words)
Angle: which teaching point or belief this is
Hook (first 3 seconds): the opening line
On-screen text: 3 to 5 short lines, in order
Script:
(the full script, written to be spoken)
Next step: the one thing they say at the end
Caption: 40 to 80 words, plus the same next step
Filming note: one line (where to stand, what to hold, what to do with hands). Optional.
```

## `reel-NN.spoken.txt`
Only the words to say, nothing else. No labels, no stage directions, no emoji, no hashtags, no brackets. One paragraph per beat. This is the file they paste into HeyGen or a teleprompter.

## How to write it
- 25 to 35 seconds when spoken: 70 to 100 words. If `config.json` or the person asks for shorter, go 15 seconds (35 to 50 words) or longer, 45 to 60 seconds (110 to 150 words).
- Write for the ear. Short sentences. Contractions. Say "you" a lot. Read it aloud in your head. Anything that trips the tongue gets rewritten.
- Hook in the first line. No warm-up, no "hey guys," no introducing themselves.
- Shape: hook, one idea, one turn or example from the source, one next step.
- Never say a number, name, or story that is not in the source.
- No "like and follow" asks unless `brand/offer.md` says so.
- Make each reel a different shape: a challenge, a quick teach, a story, a myth, a permission slip.
