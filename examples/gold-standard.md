# What good looks like

These samples show the shape, rhythm, and level of specificity to aim for. They are written about a neutral topic (starting before you feel ready). Match the quality, never copy the lines. Always write in the person's own voice from `brand/voice.md`.

## Thread opener
I used to think confidence came first.

It does not. It shows up after the third messy attempt, usually when you have stopped watching for it.

## Reel script (about 30 seconds)
You keep saying you will start when you feel ready. Okay. When is that?

Ready is a feeling, and feelings show up late. They show up after you have already moved.

So pick the smallest version. One post. One call. One page. Twenty minutes, then you are allowed to stop.

Do that today. Then tell me what you picked.

## Substack note
Nobody feels ready on day one.

They just started anyway and called it ready later.

## Email
**Subject:** The small version
**Preview:** Twenty minutes is enough

Hey,

I want to talk about the thing you have been circling.

You know the one. It sits in your notes app, and every few weeks you open it, nod, and close it again.

Here is what I have noticed. The waiting does not make it better. It just makes it heavier.

So this week, do the small version. Not the launch. The first sentence. Set a timer for twenty minutes and stop when it rings.

Reply and tell me what you picked. I read every one.

## What to notice
- Specific and plain. No grand claims, no stats, no invented stories.
- Short lines next to longer ones.
- One next step each time.
- Reads like someone talking, not like someone presenting.
