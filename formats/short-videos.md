# Short-form video spinoffs (faceless)

Videos that do not need the person on camera: voiceover over footage, text on screen, or an AI avatar. They are spinoffs of the same teaching, so they can go on TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

## Where it goes
`short-videos/video-NN.md` and `short-videos/video-NN.spoken.txt`

## `video-NN.md`
```
# Video NN: working title
Style: one of: text-on-screen, voiceover-with-b-roll, avatar-talking, list-countdown
Length: 15 to 40 seconds
Hook (first 2 seconds): text and voice
Shot list:
1. 0:00 to 0:03 | Visual: what is on screen | Text on screen: ... | Voiceover: ...
2. ...
Music mood: one phrase (calm, building, warm, driving)
Search terms for stock footage: 3 to 6 plain terms
Caption: 30 to 60 words plus the next step
```

## `video-NN.spoken.txt`
Only the voiceover words. No labels, no timestamps.

## Rules
- 5 to 8 shots. Each is a few seconds. Text on screen is 8 words or fewer.
- Describe visuals in plain words anyone could find or film: "a woman writing in a notebook at a kitchen table", "sunrise through a window", "close up of coffee being poured". Do not name specific brands or copyrighted footage.
- Do not describe a person who exists. No celebrity likenesses.
- If the source gives a short list or myth, use `list-countdown` or text-on-screen.
- Each video uses a teaching point that no other piece uses, or a fresh angle on one.
