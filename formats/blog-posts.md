# Blog posts

SEO-minded posts for the person's website, 900 to 1,400 words.

## Where it goes
`blog/NN-<slug>.md`

## File shape
```
---
seo_title: under 60 characters, includes the main phrase people would search
meta_description: 140 to 155 characters, plain, makes someone want to click
slug: lowercase-with-dashes
keywords: main phrase; 3 to 5 related phrases
---
# Headline (can differ from seo_title)

(body)
```

## Rules
- Pick the search phrase a real person would type for this problem. Do not stuff it. Use it in the title, first 100 words, one subhead, and naturally once or twice more.
- Skimmable: short paragraphs, 3 to 5 subheads written as plain sentences or questions.
- Teaches something complete enough to be useful on its own.
- Close with one next step and one line pointing to a related topic from the material if there is one.
- Do not invent statistics, studies, or sources. If a fact needs a source and the material does not give one, leave the fact out.
- Different angle from the Substack articles on the same source.
