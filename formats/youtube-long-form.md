# YouTube titles and descriptions

Everything the person needs to publish a YouTube video made from this teaching: a long-form video (the full lesson, a Live replay, a podcast episode), and optionally a Short. One block per source.

## Where it goes
`youtube.md` in the source folder.

## Shape
```
# YouTube: working title of the source

## Long-form video
Title options: (3, each 70 characters or fewer)
1.
2.
3.
Recommended: which one and why, in one line
Description:
(the description)
Chapters: (only if the source has timestamps, otherwise write "Add chapters after you upload the video.")
Tags: (8 to 12, comma separated)
Thumbnail text: (3 options, 4 words or fewer each)
Pinned comment: (one line that starts a conversation, plus the one next step)

## Short
Title: 100 characters or fewer
Description: 1 to 2 lines, then the next step
```

## Rules
- Titles: lead with what the viewer gets, in the words she would search. Specific and honest. No all caps, no clickbait, no promised results. The three options take three different angles.
- Description: the first two lines show before "show more", so put the hook and the next step there. Then 120 to 250 words that say what the video covers, with two or three natural search phrases. Never stuff them. Then any scripture references, written out.
- Never invent chapters or timestamps. Use them only if the source file has real ones. If it does not, say so in the Chapters line.
- The next step is one line in the exact wording from `brand/offer.md`, with the real link written out in the description (YouTube links work in descriptions). Never stack two asks.
- Tags: plain phrases people search, no hashtags.
- Thumbnail text is for the thumbnail image, not the title, and never repeats the title word for word.
- Scripture quoted from the source keeps its exact words and reference. Do not add a verse the speaker did not use.
- If the source is under 800 words, write only the Short block.
