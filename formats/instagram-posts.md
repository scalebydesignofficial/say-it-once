# Instagram posts and Stories

Text-led captions for single images or Reels, and short Story sequences.

## Where it goes
`instagram-posts.md`, all items in one file, separated by `---`.

## Part A: posts
```
## Post 1: working title
Best paired with: a quote graphic, a Reel, or a carousel slide (name which one)
Caption:
(the caption)
```
- 100 to 220 words. The first line is the hook and must work alone, because only the first lines show before "more."
- Short paragraphs with line breaks. Specific, plain, in the person's voice.
- One next step at the end, from `brand/offer.md`.
- Hashtags only if `brand/profile.md` says they use them: 3 to 5 at the end.
- Each post a different shape: a truth, a confession, a myth, a permission slip, a how-to, a question.

## Part B: Story sequences
```
## Story sequence 1: working title
Frame 1: text on screen (12 words or fewer) | Sticker idea: poll / question box / slider / none
Frame 2: ...
Frame 4: next step frame
```
- 3 to 5 frames per sequence. Frame 1 asks or states something that makes people tap.
- Use a sticker idea on at least one frame (poll, question box, quiz, emoji slider), written as a plain suggestion.
- Last frame holds the one next step.
